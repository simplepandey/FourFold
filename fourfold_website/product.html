Product 02 · Tank-to-Tank Controller

Tank-to-Tank Automatic Controller

Automates water transfer between a source tank and a destination tank with intelligent level-based pump control. No manual monitoring. No unnecessary pump operation. No tank overflow.

Suitable for: Residential houses, bungalows, apartments, high-rise & commercial buildings.

  • Fully automatic operation — starts and stops the transfer pump according to the destination tank's water level.
  • Dual tank monitoring — watches both the source and destination tank for safe, reliable transfer.
  • Dry-run & overflow protection — stops the pump if the source runs low, or once the destination is full.
  • Auto / Manual mode — automatic by tank level, or user-controlled for maintenance and testing.

Product 03 · Borewell Panel

Borewell Pump Control & Protection Panel

A reliable automatic control panel for borewell and submersible pumps, providing scheduled ON/OFF operation with comprehensive electrical and motor protection. The pump runs on a programmed ON TIME / OFF TIME cycle, with an upper-tank level sensor completing the control loop.

  • Timer-based automatic operation — set the ON TIME and OFF TIME; the controller runs the cycle automatically.
  • Upper tank sensor included — works alongside the programmed timer for reliable, predictable filling.
  • Complete pump protection — overload, short-circuit and dry-run / low-current protection.
  • Manual mode — direct START / STOP control for maintenance, testing and commissioning.

Installation & Operation Guide

Up and running in six steps

The wiring, keypad and setup steps below apply to the Manual Panel (Product 01) and the Borewell Timer Panel (Product 03) — both share the same physical panel, keypad and display.

Mount the panel

Mount the panel securely on the wall, at a location convenient for viewing the display and operating the keypad.

Turn OFF main power

Turn OFF the main power supply before wiring — never wire a live panel.

Open the panel

Open the panel to access the terminal block.

Connect motor & supply

Connect the Motor and Power Supply to the correct terminals as shown in the wiring diagram below.

Switch ON

Switch ON the power supply once every connection is double-checked.

Ready to operate

The system is now ready for operation — press ON to start the motor.

6 · Auto & Manual Current Setting

Let it learn, or set it yourself

Recommended

Auto Mode

In Auto Mode, the controller automatically learns and sets:

  • ✅ Overload (OL) Current
  • ✅ Dry Run (DR) Current

No manual calibration is required.

Manual Mode

1. Press & hold OL or DR until 0000 appears on the display.
2. Press OL to set Overload, or DR to set Dry Run current.
3. Use UP Arrow 1 to change the value before the decimal point.
4. Use UP Arrow 2 to change the value after the decimal point.
5. Wait a few seconds — the controller automatically saves your setting.
